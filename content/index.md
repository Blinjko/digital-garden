Welcome to my site, an artifact of myself. Here you will find various things that I have curated and created over time. The main goal of this place is to house my knowledge and the things that I've done over the years; mainly so I don't forget and also in case I need to reference things from the past. In a way it is my small little corner in a large noisy world, but here I am free to do as I please and be myself. My hope is that this site will ooze personality and have its own unique feel to it, making it feel distinctly "me". 

Take your time and peruse around the site. There are [[Worbench|Projects]] that I've worked on and that am currently working on, and there is technical knowledge stored here as well. For a reference on the structure and organization of the site see [[Organization Structure]].

In case it wasn't obvious by the name of the site, my name is Logan. Currently I am an undergraduate student studying Electrical Engineering, but I aim to be a real engineer or scientist / researcher one day. Matter of fact, one reason I ended up creating this site is because of the overwhelming amount of knowledge I was acquiring in school. I needed somewhere to put all my notes from class where I could I easily access and reference them. Obsidian seemed like a useful tool for this, plus it had LaTex support, so I took my notes in Obsidian. Eventually I fell into the digital garden rabbit hole and realized I quite enjoyed the idea of having my own little space on the internet, "Working with the garage door open" as some would say.

Regardless, many of the digital gardens I see are either from more creative minded people, like artists, writers, or from data engineers for some reason. I figured it would be a nice touch if there was a deep digital garden with roots in science, engineering, and technical subjects. I dunno, I think there is something super interesting and cool about peeking into what interesting people do with there time. And, in a similar way, I hope I can create that feeling in others. I hope that this site will inspire people and show people just how cool engineering and science really is.

Anyways, I could yap all day. Enjoy the site, peruse around, look at some of my projects maybe you'll find them interesting. Either way, I hope that you enjoy your time here and find something interesting or useful.
