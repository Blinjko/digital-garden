1. Extreme close up - self explanatory, pertains to face
2. Close up - less extreme than above
3. Medium shot - waist or chest and up
4. cowboy shot / 3/4 shot / american shot - Shot from the knees up, be careful can get cowboy like styling as well, tends to be used in anime models
5. Full shot / full frame / full body - Self explanatory
6. Wide shot - used with landscape dimensions, longer cowboy shot
7. Dutch angle / dutch tilt - gives camera tilt effect, more prominent in landscape
8. High angle shot - top down view
9. Low angle shot - floor looking up, tends to struggle with it
10. Bokeh - basically blurrs the lights to circles in the background
11. Depth of field - blurr background, not as significant as bokeh
