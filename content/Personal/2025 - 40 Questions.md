1. What did you do this year that you’d never done before?
Other than going to University and finally starting new classes in my subject, I think I did quite a few new things this year. 2 big events come to mind and they are both travel related. First I went to Greece for my Annual Training with the National Guard. It was my first time leaving the country and it was interesting getting to see different places other than America for once. I didn't really do much of note in Greece since I was confined to an Army base there, but what I do remember though is driving through Greece in the early morning and seeing the sun rise in their landscape. The mountains were so beautiful and the land had a Mediterranean look I had never seen before. There is also fond memories of people in my unit that are now gone, not dead just I don't see them anymore.

The other big travel thing I did this year was go on a cross country road trip with the old man. It started out as a silly idea but it turned into a reality and it was an interesting and insightful experience. We essentially rented a van and did a whole loop around the US, stopping at national parks we passed. IIR we stopped at Glacier NP in Montana, Crater Lake in Oregon, Olympia in Washington, and Sequoia in California. It was a sad reality check that made me change my view of the country and my thoughts about the future, but I that that is good in its own way.
