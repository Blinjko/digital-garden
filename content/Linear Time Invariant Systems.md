A linear time invariant system is a [[Systems|system]] that obeys [[Time Invariant Systems|time invariance]], and [[Linear Systems| linear]] properties of systems.

## Linear Differential Equations as LTI Systems
[[Linear Differential Equations]] are one way to describe an LTI system. Creating a differential equation to describe an LTI system is another topic in it of itself, but when given a Linear ODE we can solve it using the typical methods employed in mathematics. 

As engineers we tend to add a bit of flair and alternate interpretations of the constructs we use in solving differential equations, and I figured they are worth mentioning. In particular I am referring to the concept of a [[Transfer Function]] and [[Impulse Response]]. The names are different then what would be typically found in mathematical literature, but since that is what I know them as, that is what I will call them.
