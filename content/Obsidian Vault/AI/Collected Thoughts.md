#### Physical Symbol System Hypothesis -> [wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physical_symbol_system)
A physical symbol system has the necessary and sufficient means to create intelligence and intelligent action.

A physical symbol system is a system that uses high level symbols and manipulates them to create other symbols.

Conventional symbols are not always symbols in a PSS, for example binary signals can be considered symbolic however they are without direct meaning in it of themselves. Same goes for matrices of numbers seen in a neural net, they are technically symbols but they any known meaning on a high level, which is primarily what the PSSH is concerned with.

PSSH speaks about general intelligence rather than narrow intelligence, which is what compromises most AI currently.

There is also a distinction between intelligence and consciousness, the PSSH only speaks about intelligence and not consciousness.

As a side note this is what I have thought about myself and it is nice to see that this was thought about before.

Unsurprisingly there are many criticisms of the PSSH and many have valid rooting in their reasoning. Look at the wiki for a reference of all of them but I'll list some interesting ones here. 
- The human brain is not manipulating symbols but rather just the result of chemical reactions and that the complex behavior exhibited is akin to that of an ant colony. In a sense the brain is mindless.
- The idea that most intelligent actions and human abilities do not come from the conscious thought and actions but rather unconscious intuition which doesn't seem to appear to be using symbolic manipulation like the conscious mind.
- Sub symbolic systems, like neural nets, can generate intelligent programs and currently have outperformed symbol manipulation based AI.
- Rodney Brooks was able to create robots that did not use any form of symbolic reasoning that were superior to ones that did.

The above ideas I find particularly interesting because I have not thought about AI in this manner before. #### Hubert Dreyfus on artificial intelligence -> [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hubert_Dreyfus%27s_views_on_artificial_intelligence)
Hubert had very critical views on artificial intelligence and the running philosophy in the 70s and 80s. The main refutal relies in the idea that the intelligence exhibited by humans cannot be solely captured by symbols and symbol manipulation as previously thought. Also, he mentions how the human brain is not similarly functioning to a computer in its literal sense, and that there is an assumption that science and mathematics will be able to explain the universe as we know it, when really there is no real truth or way to prove this. The argument of the unconscious mind not being to be captured symbolically is found here as well. Hubert says that most of the time human behavior and "automatic decisions" rely on ones intuition and subconscious processes and not a chain of logical thinking and problem solving. Also these decisions of the subconscious are almost instantaneous and are based on the senses of the person at the time, not necessarily a learned pattern and procedure. The final refute Hubert mentions is the assumptions AI researchers make that all knowledge can be formalized as some sort of symbol, however there are many cases of human knowledge that are not in the form of symbols and have not been symbolized. More modern methods of AI do not use symbols and the idea of symbolic logic but not really because of Hubert's statements. Many of the ideas that Hubert had seemed to be true, but not all of them. Modern AI is using sub-symbolic, or hybrid architectures that use data driven approaches which is thought to be similar to how the human subconscious works, and has had great success compared to older symbolic based methods.

#### Situated Based Approach to AI
Also known as the bottom up approach, AI built in this manner put less emphasis on abstract reasoning ad more emphasis on reacting to a real environment that can be sensed, hence the situated part. Situated AI are able to sense their environment and react to their environment and goal is to do this in an intelligent manner, usually without using high level logic based methods. 


#### Machine Learning -> [wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Machine_learning)
A sub field of artificial intelligence based in statistical methods to learn from and generalize data in order to make future predictions. #### Genetic Programming & Algorithms -> [wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genetic_programming)
Genetic algorithms are algorithms that employ the ideas of evolution in order to find an optimal solution, in essence they are a form of optimization algorithm. They are used in engineering, like designing antennas, and few other places. They tend to not work well with complex problems, and most of the time there is a better solution, or technique, to be used in order to solve the optimization problem.

Genetic programming on the other hand is way more interesting. Genetic programming is concerned with evolving programs themselves in a genetic like nature; in essence it is evolving code. It seems that GP is more popular than GA and has been able to create many programs that are better than human written counterparts.
