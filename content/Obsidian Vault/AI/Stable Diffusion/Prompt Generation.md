
### General Advice
1. Use precise language, "fish tank" will yeild different results than "aquarium" though they seemingly mean the same thing
2. Think about the terms in the prompt at the logical level they are, taking care to not misalign them
3. Think about what the image in your mind is visually
4. Try to understand what SD will blend together, things that fall on a metaphorical "logical layer" or similar scope.
5. The position of a term in the prompt has significant weight on its own, and simply changing the position can change the results. This is because the prompt is processed in chunks and sometimes pieces fit into a chunk or get pushed out, so you want to get the pieces that pertain to each other in the same chunk.
6. Focus on completion of the image first. Put most important words / prompt entities first. Generate; then add a little detail, this helps determine what might be causing undesirable effects.
7. Use photography terms
8. Try not to use conflicting terms and concepts ( esepecially when using photography terms ). I.e dont put shoes & say close up of the face.


### Prompt Weights
- Look like "(blue eyes:1.0)"
- Value of 1.0 is equal to the plain term, the example above would be equal to "blue eyes"
- To specify a higher weight, increase the value, lower, lower it.
