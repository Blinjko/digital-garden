### Adetailer
- essentially restore faces on steroids
- uses in painting and a given prompt to alter your images eyes, hands, faces, and other details stable diffusion usually struggles with.
- Does not effect the ordering of the original prompt as it is applied AFTER everything is generated
- Works well for hard to generate features, hands, eyes, faces, etc
- link: https://github.com/Bing-su/adetailer
