Differential equations are defined as equations that involve both a function and its derivatives. For example
$$
y'(t) +y(t)=0
$$
Is a differential equation.

There are 2 main classes of differential equations
- Ordinary Differential Equations (ODE's)
- Partial Differential Equations (PDE's)

## Ordinary Differential Equations (ODE's)
ODE's are differential equations that only involve derivatives of one variable. It is the same ideas as normal derivatives versus partial derivatives. An ODE only has normal derivatives in it. Note that ODE's can be [[Linear Differential Equations|linear]] or [[Non-Linear Differential Equations|non linear]], and can involve derivatives larger than the first.

### ODE Order
The order of an ODE is similar to the order of a polynomial, except here the exponent is the derivative number so-to-speak. But in essence, the order of and ODE is defined as the highest number derivative it involves. Also since an ODE is an equation, there is a left and right side, sometimes called the output and input, and each has their own order. So if you are told that the output has an order of 3, then they are likely saying that one side has an order of 3, not the whole equation. It can be the case that both sides of the equation involve derivatives and that their order's are not the same.

## Partial Differential Equations (PDE's)
PDE's are equations that involve partial derivatives, rather than standard derivatives. An example of this could be the spacial derivatives, I.E what is the rate of change of some quantity with respect to each axis in space?


## Solving Differential Equations
Solving differential equation means finding a given input and output function that satisfy the equation. Many differential equations do not have analytical solutions, so they have to be computed numerically, but there are a few types that do have analytical solutions. As for how to solve them, that depends heavily on the equation you are working with, as the techniques are specific to certain equation styles and structures.
