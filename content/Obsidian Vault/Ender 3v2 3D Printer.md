## Bed leveling
To raise the bed up ( closer to the extruder ) turn the knobs clockwise. To lower the bed, turn them counter clockwise. Also do note that the middle of the bed acts as a fulcrum, so when one corner is raised / lowered, the diagonally opposing one is lowered / raised respectively. An easy way to tell if the bed is level is to run a bed leveling print, this usually consist of printing squares with a thickness of 1 layer on the bed. The squares are located in each corner and the center. If the squares have visible lines and lack cohesiveness then the bed is to low. If the squares are rough feeling and it almost seems as if something was dragged through them, the bed is too high.
