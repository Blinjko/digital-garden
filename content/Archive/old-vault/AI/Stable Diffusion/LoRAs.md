- To use LoRAs add their respective variables to the prompt, they look like so, "<lora:name_here:0.1>", the end 0.1 is the weight in this case.




# List of LoRAs

[Detail Tweaker](https://civitai.com/models/58390?modelVersionId=62833) - An absurdly good LoRA. If you aren't using this, you are generating images on hard mode. Adds or removes little details from your images. If I could only choose one LoRA, it would be this one. - **prompt "<lora:more_details:1.0>"**

[LowRA](https://civitai.com/models/48139?modelVersionId=63006) - Stable Diffusion really likes bright images. Sometimes you don't want bright images. In those cases, you use LowRA. Here is the base image with LowRA set to 0.7. - **prompt "<lora:lowRA:1.0>"**
