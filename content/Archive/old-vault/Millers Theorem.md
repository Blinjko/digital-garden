Millers Theorem is a circuit theorem that allows us to split a single impedance between 2 terminals into 2 different impedance between the 2 terminals and ground.

This is better explained visually so look at this picture.
![[PXL_20251125_143500495.jpg]]

The Formulas written out are
$$
Z_{1}=\frac{Z}{1-k}
$$
$$
Z_{2}=\frac{Z}{1-\frac{1}{k}}
$$
Assuming
$$
V_{2}=kV_{1}
$$
