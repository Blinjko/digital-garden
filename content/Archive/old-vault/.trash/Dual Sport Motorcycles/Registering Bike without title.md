
## In CT
If the bike is newer than 20 years a title is needed along with the various other documents.
If a bike is older than 20 years, bought in CT, a title isn't needed but the previous CT registration is needed, along with the bill of sale.

## In Vermont
It is easier to register older motorcylces 15+ years old without any documentation as long as they're under 500CC, once they go over that amount you have to get it inspected which can be a hassle. If not though, some simple parts on the registration form need completing along with a verification of the odometer reading. **NOTE** do these with the seller before taking it, as the documents require information & signatures from the buyer and seller.
