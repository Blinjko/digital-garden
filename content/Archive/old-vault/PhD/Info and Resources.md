[Reddit Post](https://www.reddit.com/r/gradadmissions/comments/n3d8st/guide_how_to_find_programs_that_fit_your_interests/) On how to search for schools.

TLDR; 
- Determine what you want to study
- Determine what you want to do after PhD, academia or industry different programs will tend to different sides
- Determine if you want a more free form program (lets you pursue various classes and interests), or a more regimented program.
- Look at state schools around where you live, or places you would like to live. Review their programs and note ones that could have potential.
- When you have some that seem intriguing, dive a bit deeper into them and reach out to people at that school.
Other Advice
- Consider places that authors and people you look up to came from, i.e where they got their PhD.
- Your interest might overlap with other departments and they might have a different program that fits you better.
- Don't go to a school that doesn't study, or have anyone studying the subject your interested in.
- Ask professors and other people working on things your interested in, they might have advice and what schools to go to.
