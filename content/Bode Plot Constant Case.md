The constant case refers to the constant coefficient of our [[Transfer Function]], $H(s)$. I will denote it as $C$.

For example if we have
$$
H(s) = \frac{10}{\frac{s}{a}+1}
$$
Then $C=10$. Do note that the [[Transfer Function]] has been [[Normalize Transfer Function|normalized]].

## Magnitude Contribution
The constant $C$ has no effect of the magnitude contribution.


## Phase Contribution

If
- $C > 0 \implies$ no phase contribution
- $C< 0 \implies$ $+\pi$ contribution. Can think of this as an overall offset.
