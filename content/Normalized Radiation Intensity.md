This term comes up in the discussion of [[Antennas]] and is defined as
$$
F(\theta,\phi)=\frac{S(R,\theta,\phi)}{S_{MAX}}
$$
It is also referred to as the radiation pattern.
$\theta$ and $\phi$ are the variables for [[Spherical Coordinates]], where $\theta$ is the angle measured from the +Z axis and $\phi$ is the angle from the +X axis.

## Elevation Pattern
Is defined as the plot of $F(\theta)$ .
Note that $\theta=90$ is defined as the broadside direction.

## Azimuth Pattern / H pattern
Is defined as the plot of $F(\phi)$.
